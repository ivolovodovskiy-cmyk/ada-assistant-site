Ada: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who uses Ada

Ada is a private personal assistant with one user, its owner. It is not available to the public.

What data Ada accesses

With the owner's consent, Ada accesses the owner's own Google account data: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests, such as reading and organising email, checking the calendar, and reading or editing the owner's documents.

Sharing

Ada does not sell Google user data and does not share it with third parties, except with service providers that process the owner's requests on the owner's behalf. Data is not used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Access credentials are stored on a private server controlled by the owner. The owner can revoke Ada's access at any time in their Google Account settings, which stops all access.

Google API Services

Ada's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
